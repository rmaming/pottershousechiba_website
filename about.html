Pastor Jojo & Milky

Pastor Jojo and Milky are from the Philippines.

Milky was born into a Christian family and is the daughter of a pastor. Pastor Jojo was first invited to church by Milky, and it was there that he encountered the Lord and began a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

After they were married, Pastor Jojo served as a pastor in a local church in the Philippines for three years, with the support and partnership of Milky in ministry and service. During this time, through prayer and seeking God's guidance, they came to understand God's calling to draw the people of Japan closer to Him, so that the love and light of the Lord may reach the Japanese people.

They were sent as missionaries by their mother church, PHCC Lucena City, Philippines, to serve in Japan.

Pastor Jojo is a pastor by calling, and both Pastor Jojo and Milky are computer engineers by profession. They believe that God works through sincere relationships, faithful living, and a quiet but consistent witness.

Their prayer is that more people in Japan will come to know Jesus, grow in a personal relationship with Him, and experience His love, light, goodness, and faithfulness in their lives.
